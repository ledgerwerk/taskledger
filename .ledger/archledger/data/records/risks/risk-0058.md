The current storage layout is v6 (`TASKLEDGER_STORAGE_LAYOUT_VERSION` in `taskledger/domain/states.py`). Layout-5-to-6 migration introduces a UUIDv7 identity inventory, deterministic conversion of numeric task bundles, and derived aliases; incomplete or mixed states must remain recoverable and must not reuse identities. Mitigation: migration performs safety preflight and preserves recovery data, read-only access does not migrate, and doctor checks validate layout and identity consistency. Future format changes must maintain backward compatibility or provide explicit migration steps.
